About

I'm a PhD student and research assistant in the School of Plant, Environmental, and Soil Sciences at LSU. My work brings together soil physics, field sensor data, satellite observations, and a good amount of code to understand how water moves and is stored in soil — from a single profile up to regional scales.

Before coming to LSU, I completed an Erasmus Mundus MSc in Soils and Global Change at Ghent University and Aarhus University. My BSc is in Natural Resources Management from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology in Ghana.

Research

Surface and root-zone connections

How well does near-surface soil moisture reflect what is happening deeper down, and how long does the soil hold on to a wet or dry spell?

Satellite remote sensing

Pairing satellite soil moisture and thermal observations with ground measurements so these products can be used with more confidence in hydrology.

Drought and agriculture

Building soil moisture indicators that are easy to interpret, for drought monitoring and practical decisions in agriculture.
