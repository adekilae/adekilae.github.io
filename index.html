About me

I am a PhD student and research assistant in the School of Plant, Environmental, and Soil Sciences at Louisiana State University. My research integrates soil physics, in-situ observations, satellite remote sensing, and computational methods to understand soil-water dynamics across scales.

My academic background includes an Erasmus Mundus MSc in Soils and Global Change at Ghent University and Aarhus University, and a BSc in Natural Resources Management from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology, Ghana.

Research interests

01

Surface–root-zone connectivity

Understanding how near-surface soil moisture observations relate to deeper soil-water storage and memory.

02

Satellite remote sensing

Integrating satellite soil moisture and thermal observations with ground measurements for hydrologic applications.

03

Drought & precision agriculture

Developing interpretable soil moisture indicators to support drought monitoring and agricultural decisions.
